ABOUT US

A simple place to learn better.

LearnSphere Mentoria provides home tuition for school and PUC students with an emphasis on understanding concepts clearly.

We believe learning becomes easier when students can ask questions freely, understand concepts step by step and study in a comfortable environment.

Our tuition support is designed for students from 1st Standard to 10th Standard and 1st & 2nd PUC.

LearnSphere Mentoria is a new home tuition initiative based in Bengaluru.

Our approach is focused on clear explanations, regular learning and building confidence in students.
